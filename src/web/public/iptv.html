SILENT CHANNELS — loads of IPTV feeds carry AC-3 or MP2 audio, which no browser can decode; you get picture and nothing else. The LCD shows NO AUD and the status bar names the codec. SCAN tags them NOAUD where the playlist advertises its codecs, and SEL SILENT ticks the lot. VLC plays these fine — it's a browser limit, not a broken stream.
AUD↻ rebuilds the audio pipeline if everything has gone quiet.
FULL and MAX — FULL is the picture only, MAX is the whole app including the playlist. Pressing one while you are in the other swaps straight over; pressing the one you are already in comes back to the window. In FULL, changing channel flashes the channel and what is on it for a few seconds, so you can see where you landed without waking the control bar.
TV GUIDE — the guide panel now says what the download actually gave you: "listings for 181 of your 225 channels". If a download arrives cut short, or covers channels that are not yours, or is out of date, it is rejected and the guide you already had is kept — with a line saying which of the three it was. A guide that works is never replaced by one that does not.
SKINS — press K or the SKIN button. Seven looks: the classic grey and green, an amber monitor, ice blue, plasma, a walnut Bakelite radio, olive tactical, and a light DAYLIGHT skin for a bright room. The phosphor colour drives the CRT tint and the level meter too, so the whole thing moves together. Your CRT, GPU and audio settings are not touched.
GPU FILTERS — press V. DEBLOCK and DEBAND clean up compression damage at the stream's own resolution, then SHARPEN (contrast-adaptive, so it won't halo) and the colour controls run at your display resolution. Start with RESCUE for a typical low-bitrate feed. B or A/B flips to the raw picture so you can see what it's actually doing. Frame interpolation is not possible in a browser — run the player fullscreen and point Lossless Scaling at the window for that.
GROUPS — press GROUPS (or G) for a list of every group with a tick box and a channel count. Tick as many as you like, shift-click for a range, then DELETE wipes all of their channels in one go. Click a group's name to open it in the channel list instead. UNDO works here too.
CLEANUP — press EDIT. Click rows to tick them, shift-click for a range, or filter by group first and use SEL ALL. SCAN tests every listed stream: OK plays, DEAD is gone, CORS is alive but refuses browser playback, UNSUP is a format no browser can decode (raw .ts, rtmp, rtsp, .mpd). SEL FAILED ticks all three, DELETE removes them, SAVE .M3U writes the cleaned file to your Downloads while the app keeps playing the cleaned list.
NOTES — double-click a titlebar to roll the window up. Click the analyzer to switch between the simulated meter and the real audio spectrum. Playlists loaded by URL need the host to allow cross-origin requests; if it fails, download the .m3u and open it from disk instead.
