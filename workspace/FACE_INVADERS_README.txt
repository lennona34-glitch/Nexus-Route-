CONTROLS:
---------
• WASD - Move your ship in 3D space
• Mouse - Aim your weapons
• Left Click (Hold) - Fire primary weapon
• Space - Fire special Nova Bomb
• Mouse cursor shows targeting crosshair

------------
1. NORMAL (Red) - 5 HP, 50 points

   - Moderate speed and firepower

2. HEAVY (Orange) - 15 HP, 100 points
   - Larger hexagonal enemies
   - Tougher and more aggressive

3. BOSS (Magenta) - 50 HP, 500 points
   - Octagonal super enemies
   - High damage output
   - Appears every 5th wave

--------
1. PULSE CANNON (Cyan)
   - Single powerful shots
   - 5 damage per hit
   - Good all-around weapon

2. SPREAD SHOT (Yellow)
   - Triple shot spread pattern
   - 3 damage per projectile
   - Great for crowd control

3. LASER (Magenta)
   - Fast, piercing beams
   - 8 damage per hit
   - High damage output

4. NOVA BOMB (White) - Special Weapon
   - 360-degree explosion
   - 10 damage per projectile
   - Limited ammo (starts with 3)
   - Collect power-ups to refill
